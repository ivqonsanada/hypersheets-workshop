# Writing the lines

The video is read on a phone, in a few seconds per scene. Every line must be true, short and easy to say aloud.

## Where the words come from

- The brief, the user's files, the user's answers. Nothing else.
- A page's `index.html` holds most of what a promo needs: the name (`<h1>`), the line under it, the lists, the address if it is written there. Copy spellings letter for letter.
- If the address is not in the brief or the files, ask the user once. Never guess one, and never write `example.com`.
- No number without a source in the brief. No quote unless the brief has the person's own words and their name. When in doubt, use a `list` instead of a `stat` or a `quote`.

## The headline is the hook

The first frame is the cover: its headline decides whether anyone watches the rest.

- Say what the video is about in the fewest words: the offer ("Diskon 25% semua kemeja batik"), the news ("Kelas baru mulai 5 Januari 2027"), the question ("Ini true atau false?"), the result ("Sepatu putih kusam, bersih lagi").
- A name alone is a headline only when the video introduces that person or shop and the brief gives nothing more pointed. Otherwise the name goes in the `kicker` and on the end card.
- The `kicker` adds something the headline does not say: who, where or when ("Laundry Kinclong", "Sabtu, 13 Desember 2026"). Not the headline's own words again, and not a greeting ("Selamat datang di"). `Halo, aku` and `Hi, I am` go only above a person's name.
- The `sub` adds one more fact. It is not a list of what the video will show.

## How a line should read

- One idea per line. A line is a phrase, not a paragraph: "Roti hangat tiap Sabtu pagi", not "Kami menyediakan berbagai macam roti yang hangat".
- Say the thing, not that it is great. "Bisa diambil mulai pukul sembilan" beats "Pelayanan terbaik untuk Anda".
- No filler words: terbaik, berkualitas, solusi, inovatif, unlock, seamless, next level, game changer.
- Sentence case. No full stop at the end of a headline, a list line or a button. A `sub`, a `detail` or a `caption` may be a full sentence without the final full stop.
- No emoji, no hashtag, no dash as punctuation. Use a comma, or two lines.
- List lines start the same way: all with a noun ("Daftar harga", "Jam buka", "Peta lokasi") or all with a verb ("Pilih rotinya", "Kirim pesan", "Ambil Sabtu").
- A button is two or three words that start with a verb: "Buka halamannya", "Pesan sekarang", "Open the page".
- On the end card the `line` and the `button` say two different things: the line gives the reason or the deadline ("Pesan untuk Sabtu ini"), the button the action ("Chat lewat WA"). When the address is a phone number, one of them says what it is for ("Chat lewat WA", "Telepon kami").
- Each fact once. If the title already gives the date, the list does not give it again.
- A heading fits every line under it. Read the heading before each line: "Yang kami sediakan: berlaku sampai Minggu" is wrong, so that line belongs in another scene.
- No filling. "Kirim pesan lewat WhatsApp" under "Chat WA ke 0811-0000-619" says nothing new: leave the `detail` out.

## The voice

- Indonesian: follow the brief or the page. If it says `aku`, write `aku`; if it says `saya`, write `saya`; a shop usually says `kami`. Do not mix them. Do not translate product names.
- English: plain words, `I` for a person, `we` for a shop.
- Never mix the two languages in one video, except names and code.

## When a line is too long

The builder tells you by how many characters. Shorten in one of these two ways, and only these:

1. **Cut before a joining word and keep the words before it, word for word.** Joining words: `yang`, `dengan`, `untuk`, `supaya`, `agar`, `tanpa`, `sambil`; `that`, `which`, `with`, `so`, `because`, `without`, `while`.
   - "Belajar membuat formulir pendaftaran yang mudah diisi di HP" becomes "Belajar membuat formulir pendaftaran"
   - "Writing tests for my shop API so it never breaks again" becomes "Writing tests for my shop API"
2. **Write the whole line again as a new short sentence** that says the same thing.

Never take single words out of the middle and keep the rest: "Formulir pendaftaran mudah HP" is wrong. Read the shortened line aloud; if nobody would say it that way, use the first way.

## Ready-made lines

| Field | Indonesian | English |
|---|---|---|
| title `kicker` for a person | `Halo, aku` or `Halo, saya` | `Hi, I am` |
| page `title` | `Ini halamanku`, `Ini halaman kami` | `This is my page`, `This is our page` |
| list `heading` | `Yang sedang kupelajari`, `Yang kami sediakan` | `What I am learning`, `What we offer` |
| steps `heading` | `Caranya`, `Cara pesan` | `How it works`, `How to order` |
| compare `label` | `Dulu` and `Sekarang`, `Salah` and `Benar` | `Before` and `After`, `Wrong` and `Right` |
| end `line` | `Mampir ke halamanku`, `Pesan sekarang` | `Come and see my page`, `Order today` |
| end `button` | `Buka halamannya` | `Open the page` |
